About Us

Welcome to Dadpur Young Star Association Club! We are a community-based cultural organization devoted to preserving traditions and promoting togetherness through the grand celebration of Jagadhatri Puja in Dadpur. Every year, our club brings people of all ages together to experience the spirit of devotion, culture, and unity. Since our founding, Dadpur Young Star Association Club has proudly organized Jagadhatri Puja with dedication and creativity. From majestic idols and artistic pandals to cultural events, community feasts (bhog), and social outreach — we aim to make the festival not just a celebration, but a heartfelt experience for all. More than just a club, we are a family — built on shared values, mutual respect, and a love for tradition. Through our efforts, we strive to strengthen the cultural roots of our community and pass them on to future generations. Join us in celebrating Jagadhatri Puja — where devotion meets tradition, and community becomes family.
